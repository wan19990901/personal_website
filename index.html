Research

Research Interests

My work connects agent design, test-time reasoning, and post-training to make capable AI systems more reliable and efficient over long horizons.

01

Self-improving agents

Agents that learn from successful and failed trajectories, refine reusable skills, and improve how they approach repeated tasks.

Auto-researchLong-horizon agents
02

Efficient reasoning

Adaptive test-time compute, verification, and sampling strategies that spend inference resources only when they are useful.

ReasoningOptimal stopping
03

Trustworthy AI

Methods and evaluations for reliable, fair, and empathetic AI across language, healthcare, and real-world decision settings.
